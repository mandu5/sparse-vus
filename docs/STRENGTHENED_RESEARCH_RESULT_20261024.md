## Research contribution

For existing dynamic VUS and weighted range precision/recall, derive the local
statistics sufficient to maintain the exact evaluation query as thresholds
change, and separate those identities from compilation and conventional reuse.
The weighted operator tracks fixed-label overlap, fragmentation and moving
positional normalization. Classical component trees, sorting, prefix sums,
closed-form interval overlap and offline bucketing are credited antecedents.
The new claim is the explicit coupled metric state and its consequences, not
the invention of those techniques.

The finite-feature theorem states cardinality-evaluation cost explicitly.
The canonical complete query-associated curve takes O(n log n) arithmetic
operations and matches a comparison-model lower bound. The standard sorting
reduction is not a new lower-bound technique. It does not cover scalar-only
queries, unrestricted algebraic computation or fixed-width radix algorithms.
The implementation covers four canonical positional biases and three
cardinality factors; the more general finite-feature family is theorem-only.

## Boundaries reached

- MSL id7 loses against the faster conventional control on both hosts:
  .563 times on Mac and .684 times on Linux. Its few distinct queries make
  repeated scanning competitive. A narrow aggregate timing interval does not
  remove this recording-level loss.
- On the separate public-detector panel, maximum selection regret is only
  .001412, with no case reaching the prespecified .01 practical cutoff.
  The earlier 15/73 disjoint winner-set result cannot become a prevalence
  claim about the new detectors or unseen data.
- Default combined TimeEval gains over the faster compiled control are small
  and not consistently supported across executions. The earlier nine-metric VUS-only replacement remains
  **1.031 times** end to end. Its unfavorable result is retained.
- Mac and Linux regenerate their own score outputs. All FFT profile hashes
  agree, while 25 COPOD hashes differ. Inputs, query counts and timing
  uncertainty are reported by host; this is not an isolated CPU intervention.
- Censored public calls do not have completed timings or verified outputs.
  Compiled-control parity does not replace a missing public-reference output.
- The contribution remains a specialized exact analytics operator. This work
  does not measure an uninspected GPU evaluator or establish detector-accuracy
  SOTA. More repeats of the same panel cannot resolve conceptual significance.

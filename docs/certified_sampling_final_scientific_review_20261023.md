## What the completed results establish

1. **A narrow, mechanism-specific result survives review.** In the pinned dynamic denominator, recall can saturate before all soft mass is admitted. A retained later threshold can move that mass's higher precision onto an earlier recall increment. The explicit proof, finite witness, and three ordinary-width ranking examples support that claim. They do not establish a novel general theory of PR interpolation.
2. **Actual default-grid decision errors exist in the selected released-score bank.** The full 128-series panel has eight best-case regrets at least 0.01 across four families, maximum 0.398575. Signed errors include both directions. The integer full-grid analysis certifies all 128 unique exhaustive winners and separates all eight above-cutoff cases from the other 120. This is stronger evidence than a few floating-point pair reversals.
3. **The dependence limits are substantial and properly visible.** Twenty endpoint-shift matches to prior physical inputs remain in the frozen primary panel. The descriptive 108 nonmatching cases contain four practical errors across two families, failing the original finite-panel gate. Therefore the numerical eight-case finding is valid, while a wholly unseen-recording or broadly representative-prevalence story is not. The reported family bootstrap interval also does not establish prevalence above five percent.
4. **Partial certification has a useful but limited output contract.** At H=250 and H=1000 it certifies 63 and 94 winners, respectively. Those are already-correct sampled choices; it certifies no wrong sampled set as suboptimal. Full-grid certification resolves every winner. The paper correctly distinguishes confirming some choices from improving the selector.
5. **The simple complete-grid remedy must remain central.** Every published score vector has at most 101 classes. Uniform rank sampling can still omit rare classes at H=250, while enumerating every score class is sufficient. The exact matched evaluator is only modestly faster than the compiled sampled control, at 1.044 geometric comparator/exact ratio. The unchanged Python comparison cannot support a fivefold intrinsic gain from exactness.
6. **The guarantee has a measured price.** The separate cost study gives partial/full certificate geometric ratio 0.815, partial/numerical-exact 2.683, and full/numerical-exact 3.290. These are measured scalar-call costs, not a certified-selection pipeline with fallback. The new recommendation to use exact numerical evaluation ordinarily and full certificates when rigorous ideal-target ordering is required follows the data. The partial-grid method is neither the fastest routine evaluator nor a demonstrated better selector.

## Required local revisions before calling the manuscript ready

**R1. Cite the official default and exact routine, not just the new third-party optimizer.** The scientific relevance should not seem to depend on a recently published Klein repository. At the immutable official revision already pinned by this project, [`vus/metrics.py`](https://github.com/TheDatumOrg/VUS/blob/467bace0c103f5c2bdb4900306d82939bcac8ff9/vus/metrics.py) defines `get_metrics(..., version='opt', ..., thre=250)`. Its `RangeAUC_volume_opt` uses the same rank grid, dynamic positive total and right-step PR rule. Cite the existing `vusreference` entry beside the semantic contract/default and retain Klein as the independent fast configured-grid control. Do not extend this to every VUS consumer or claim that MSAD's historical table used this contract.

**R2. Include PAULA in the retained-state bounds comparison.** Two sentences suffice: deterministic PR bounds from sampled ranks are established under ranking-monotonicity assumptions, while this enclosure needs no precision-monotonicity assumption and treats the stated dynamic target. Keep the mass envelope explicitly elementary. This fixes a concrete omission rather than manufacturing a broad novelty claim.

**R3. Give the PDF a direct reference to the new reproducible evidence.** The reviewed PDF cites the v0.3.0 precursor and discusses hashes, but does not itself identify a stable new artifact entry point. The new reproduction guide exists at `docs/certified_sampling_reproduction_20261023.md`; provide a stable commit/release or appropriately anonymous artifact reference for the actual submission. A moving branch and an old release are not interchangeable with the source/ledger snapshot supporting the new tables. This is a packaging requirement, not a request to rerun the completed study.

**R4. Preserve the current disclosure of the negative comparisons.** Full-grid certification is the strong same-guarantee baseline; partial certificates leave all erroneous choices unresolved; quantization and overlap restrict generalization. Those are central scientific results, not caveats to shorten away after the review. The updated practical recommendation at the end of `sampling_empirical.tex` now handles this correctly.

The public PDF is deliberately a named preprint. A TMLR submission requires its separate anonymous version and anonymous supplementary materials under the [author guidelines](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html). This is a submission-format distinction, not a scientific defect in the public preprint.

## Optional improvements, not grounds for another open-ended experiment cycle

- Add two construction sentences to the information-limit paragraph: keep every halo point in an identical common tail, choose the first point outside maximum support, and fill the rest of the first rank gap with outside-support negatives. This makes the all-width identity of retained states easier to verify without opening the exploratory notes.
- Define the reported empirical regret once as `max_j E_j - max_{j in sampled_winners} E_j`. The prose already says this, but a displayed formula makes the optimistic treatment of sampled ties immediately clear.
- Keep the huge finite `>0.98` construction visibly separate from the ordinary-width table; adding “worst-case” in the abstract is an inexpensive safeguard against a prevalence interpretation.
- A future full-precision detector-score bank would test external scope and certificate cost when the number of score classes is large. It is not a missing control for the explicitly quantized-panel claim. Likewise, more VUS semantic variants would broaden applicability but are unnecessary to prove the pinned-target result.
- Do not add an adaptive/fallback method claim without implementing and measuring that policy. The current data do not establish such a policy's advantage.

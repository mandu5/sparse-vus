## Why this is a bounded stopping decision

Extra repetitions of the same recordings cannot establish broader conceptual
novelty, application adoption, or benefits at low query counts. A different
complete consumer or a new stronger exact evaluator could extend or challenge
the operating range; it would be a new prospective study, not an excuse to
select a larger ratio from this study. A recurrence counterexample or a primary
antecedent already giving the same joint identities would require substantive
revision. These are more consequential than changing a simulated numeric score.

The fixed stopping rule is: retain the single v2 execution including negative
or incomplete evidence, independently reconstruct its results, incorporate it
without replacing earlier outcomes, freeze one final PDF, obtain three
independent reviews of that same PDF, verify the publication artifact and CMT
attachment, and complete the authorized submission. An AI review remains an
AI review; actual receipt and editorial stages are kept separate.

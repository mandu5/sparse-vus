The three substantive directions tried before the confirmatory study were
full-distinct evaluation, shared geometry/width-major batching, and partial
integer certification with exact fallback. The certificate route was 33.93 times
slower with zero early selections and is retained as a negative result. The
later operating-range objection receives three separate forms of evidence:
long real recordings, controlled factor interventions, and instrumented work
counts. A larger number of tests or files is not counted as a scientific gain.

## Major weaknesses

1. **The new study does not yet establish superiority to the strongest eligible public implementation.** Its only measured external method is Klein. The research-case document itself identifies contemporary Benchopt evaluation as pending. The reviewed older manuscript contains Benchopt and DTAI measurements, but those use different inputs and task units; they cannot be relabelled as this new comparison. Benchopt returns both ROC and PR, which must remain explicit. Comparing the cost of its available API is useful; attributing all of that ratio to PR support compression would be unjustified. The matched dense control is consequently essential even if the additional external check succeeds. The default sampled configuration also belongs on a separate accuracy-cost plot: high distinct-score cardinality alone does not show that exhaustive evaluation changes any decision on the new panel.

2. **The confidence intervals are conditional on an insufficiently characterized independence unit.** I found only **nine distinct int8 label sequences among 36 SMD series**, with one sequence shared by 18 series. Those 18 sequences also match the label bytes of excluded development series `179_SMD_id_2_Facility_tr_5925_1st_17580`. This is exact shared label geometry, not proof of identical observations or of a particular original machine mapping. It is nevertheless directly relevant to a geometry-reuse mechanism. Name-disjoint development exclusion is not geometry-disjoint confirmation. In addition, 62/73 recording names were previously measured. The protocol honestly discloses the name reuse, but the result must remain prospective new-score evidence on partly reused source data, not independent new-domain evidence.

   A small sensitivity analysis does not reverse the result. I averaged log speedups within each identical-label SMD block, gave the nine blocks equal weight, and bootstrapped those block means 10,000 times with seed 20261024. At width 30, released/stream is **1.217 [1.191, 1.246]**, and Klein/stream is **23.755 [20.631, 26.542]**. These are unadjusted 95% sensitivity intervals, not a replacement for the frozen estimand or a verified original-recording bootstrap. Their direction is reassuring, but neither label uniqueness nor this analysis establishes source independence.

3. **The incremental mechanism and its adverse tails need proportionate claims.** Most of the new gain on SMAP and SMD is already supplied by shared preparation: stream versus geometry-only has geometric ratios 1.024 and 1.025 at width 30, with 3/17 and 2/36 losses. NAB's marginal ratio is 1.094. The new batch claim is thus more modest than the 25–31x external-library ratio. Against released code, SMD's 1.207 primary geometric ratio is below the project's optional 1.25 investment threshold; the overall 73-series geometric ratio of 1.295 must not conceal that collection result. This does not make the measured gain useless or determine the venue rating mechanically.

   At width 100, stream loses to saturation on four cases: two NAB, one SMAP, one SMD. The worst ratio is 0.919 on `188_SMD_id_11_Facility_tr_500_1st_392`. It also loses to geometry-only on three SMAP cases, with minimum 0.980. These are useful regime evidence and should appear alongside the positive averages. The prior manuscript's unsuccessful default-width saturation criterion also remains part of the whole-paper evidence.

4. **Resource evidence is incomplete, and retained workspace is not public-call peak memory.** The reviewed new receipts contain one macOS arm64 run. The older whole evaluator has separate Mac/Linux results in the manuscript; that evidence does not establish cross-platform performance of the new batch code. There is no new per-method peak-memory measurement. Moreover, `np.isfinite(scores).all()` at `batch.py:92` creates an `M*n` boolean temporary, and conversion at line 88 can create a float64 `M*n` copy for other accepted inputs. Thus `O(MQ+n+Q+K)` describes retained kernel state, not the general peak auxiliary space of the public call. A safe public-call upper bound includes `Mn`. This source check was prompted by the accompanying theory review and is not claimed as an independent discovery. The timing benefit may remain entirely valid; the resource claim needs correction or separately versioned implementation work.

5. **The new evidence is not yet a cleanly reproducible principal paper result.** The driver exposes three mutating stages with fixed `OUT`/`CACHE` paths and exclusive file creation, rather than a single fresh-output reproduction entry point. Re-running `report` in the populated result directory also fails on exclusive creation of `report.json`. This preserves frozen evidence, but the reader needs a supported fresh-workspace path. The run receipt records Python/NumPy/platform, while a complete new-run manifest should also identify CPU, Numba and relevant numerical libraries/thread settings. The existing manuscript still describes the earlier studies. This is an integration requirement for the new claims, not a claim that the older artifact lacks reproduction instructions.

## Minor weaknesses and interpretation limits

- The eight procedures are useful diverse evaluator inputs, but all share trailing windows of length 16, standardization, and an at-most-2,048-window transductive reference set. They are custom fixed procedures, not a reproduction of eight tuned TSB-AD detector pipelines. The shortest/longest new series are 1,000/28,696 observations. Broad detector-quality, online-detection, and very-large-scale workload claims would need different evidence.
- The LOF procedure calls `LocalOutlierFactor(novelty=True).score_samples(data)` on data that includes its fitted reference points. Version-matched scikit-learn documentation distinguishes these novelty scores from standard training-point LOF scores. This does not invalidate VUS timing on the stored numbers, but the workload should explicitly identify this nonstandard mixed scoring convention. Preserve the frozen outputs and use a separately declared standard-LOF sensitivity study if making stronger detector-representativeness claims. [scikit-learn 1.8 documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.neighbors.LocalOutlierFactor.html).
- The single retained KMeans warning concerns only five distinct clusters for the requested 16 on `534_SMAP_id_4_Sensor_tr_1968_1st_4370`. Retaining it is correct. Report it and the real tie distribution; SMAP's median distinct-score fraction is about 0.505, so “unrounded” should not become “all distinct.”
- Workflow timing is warm-file/read-to-JSON-close timing, without detector fitting, import/JIT latency, cold storage, or `fsync`. The protocol discloses this correctly. Trial bootstrap intervals describe repeat variation on fixed collections; twelve timings do not supply twelve new datasets. The three-collection adjustment does not automatically cover every method, both widths, and all exploratory summaries.
- The order schedule balances positions but uses rotations and reversals of a fixed order. It is not evidence that all carryover effects or host drift are removed. This is more relevant to the 2–3% geometry/stream difference than to the large external gap; order/trial sensitivity would strengthen the marginal claim.

### Largest remaining weakness and concrete limits of the recommendation

The largest scientific weakness is **the conceptual breadth and significance of a specialized exact-operator improvement**. Support/cumsum reuse, standard sorting, cumulative integration and batching are established; the simplest PR restriction is a one-expression change. The defensible contribution is their rigorously specified operator reduction, particularly the global-tie/ROC treatment and avoided width work, supported by useful measured savings. The newly separated controls, long-workflow savings and explicit unsuccessful regimes make that a reasonable regular-research contribution in my judgment. They do not justify a claim of a general new query-processing primitive or superior anomaly detection.

The current scoped empirical conclusions do not require an additional experiment to repair an identified validity defect. Before making stronger claims, the following conditions are measurable:

1. For robust incremental sharing on the long collections, use a separately frozen, fully balanced workflow design with enough independent timing blocks and a declared practical effect threshold; the present three-pass intervals do not establish it.
2. For a cross-platform detector-selection or hardware-causal claim, retain and compare both hosts' generated arrays and diagnose the observed SVM discrepancy. Current records support a second execution and within-host evaluator comparisons only.
3. For the 6-point investment route, demonstrate a new mechanism meeting the predeclared all-collection threshold, or a separately designed substantive generalization beyond the released operator. Preserve the failed original SMD criterion rather than relabeling a different host, width or follow-up as the primary test.
4. Keep estimator labels explicit: a ratio of median workflow times differs from a paired geometric trial ratio. Preserve the shared-label sensitivity, three-pass qualifications, synthetic status and higher memory peaks in the submission.

The accepted public-review examples support considering a carefully scoped, useful combination rather than demanding a new general paradigm; the rejected examples still warn against vague novelty and attributing a library gap to one component. The present manuscript now responds to those actual objections. That substantive change, together with the newly observed operating boundary, is the basis for the improved recommendation.

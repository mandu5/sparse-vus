## Synopsis and venue fit

The paper preserves two existing evaluation operators while reducing repeated work across thresholds. VUS retains complete global ties and accounts for inactive ROC runs with predecessor rectangles. The stronger weighted range result maintains recall overlap/fragmentation per true interval and precision contributions per predicted component, including normalization that changes when components merge. Prefix feature sums support endpoint-dependent weights. The complete canonical trajectory costs `O(n log n)` operations under the stated comparison/arithmetic assumptions.

The target is **PVLDB Volume 20, Regular Research, Foundations and Algorithms**, November 2026 submission cycle. The official policy lists the monthly full deadline as November 1, 5 p.m. Pacific, with the mandatory abstract due October 25. This review is a scientific recommendation, not a submission receipt. The [current research call](https://www.vldb.org/2027/call-for-research-track.html) explicitly accommodates formal algorithmic contributions with prototype evaluation. The [scope policy](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html) requires a substantive data-management problem, connection to database research, and evaluation in a data-management context.

I judge the scope connection sufficient: repeated analytics over stored score columns is concrete; VUS is a substantive database-literature antecedent; the native TimeEval path measures reading stored profiles, evaluating the requested metrics and writing results. This is a specialized analytics operator, not a database system. The contribution should be judged at that scale. An unrelated detector experiment or additional database terminology would not strengthen this scope argument.

## Three strengths

1. **A precise metric-specific result with a constructive proof.** The range update handles two coupled sides of the score, including an off-label bridge that leaves recall unchanged but alters precision and its moving normalization. The finite-feature assumptions and cardinality evaluation cost are explicit. The VUS proof likewise identifies a real error in simply joining retained ROC endpoints. These are useful computational identities for existing targets.
2. **The algorithmic improvement survives serious conventional controls.** The new interval implementation uses ordered intersections and closed-form weights; the raster implementation can be faster and remains visible. Both use the same compiler. Supplemental exhaustive raster/incremental ratios are about 8.61/21.99/1.42 on Apple and 11.62/26.72/1.73 on Linux for WSD/Stock/MSL. The native exhaustive combined workload still improves 1.93–2.76 times across all four executions. These comparisons substantially weaken the explanation that the entire result is merely compilation or an unnecessarily naive overlap implementation.
3. **The evidence retains its unfavorable cases.** The paper separates the reused 73-recording panel from the separately selected 26 recordings, uses unchanged public FFT/COPOD APIs, records fresh-host score differences, keeps the MSL loss and censoring, and reports negligible default-consumer improvement and the earlier 1.031-fold nine-metric consumer result. This makes its limited success claim considerably more credible.

## Three weaknesses

1. **The conceptual novelty is moderate and narrowly specialized.** Component-tree construction, attribute updates, ordered overlap traversal, prefix moments, offline sorting/bucketing and query-associated sorting lower bounds are established tools. The new contribution is the particular coupled state and identities. Neither the general framework nor the four positional biases is new. The VUS nearest-geometry control does not establish a separate speed advantage over direct compiled ramps, and the marginal batching improvement is much smaller than the public-library gap.
2. **The demonstrated application value is concentrated in exhaustive evaluation.** At native default sampling, the incremental method provides little benefit beyond compiled raster. The separate public-detector panel has no regret at or above .01 and maximum observed regret about .001412. Native timing uses flat/cardinality-one semantics, so it does not demonstrate a deployed need for moving middle weights or reciprocal fragmentation. The original nine-metric workflow remains almost unaffected. Faster exact evaluation is valuable, but a broad claim of improved detector choice or routine end-user acceleration would be unsupported.
3. **Generality and uncertainty remain limited.** MSL has two recordings and one consistently unfavorable exhaustive case. Both new detectors are from the same public source family; the original panel contains substantial recording/label reuse. Six balanced orders establish conditional timing evidence, not a deployment population. The original and supplemental Linux native ratios differ materially despite matching score hashes; narrow within-run intervals do not cover that between-run variation. Warm execution, local data acquisition requirements and incomplete public Stock reference outputs further limit external generalization.

## Originality and importance

[Tatbul et al.](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/file/8f468c873a32bb0619eaeb2050ba45d1-Paper.pdf) already introduce positional and cardinality weighting and optimize one-mask evaluation using ordered ranges and closed forms. [Menotti et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228747458_1D_Component_tree_in_linear_time_and_space_and_its_application_to_gray-level_image_multithresholding) explicitly compute component attributes without changing construction complexity. [Ranefall and Wählby](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cyto.a.22806) already compute threshold-wise precision/recall using component-size attributes. A broad claim to invent threshold trajectories, attributes, or precision/recall from component trees would therefore fail.

The manuscript makes a narrower and defensible distinction. Ranefall's positive mass is defined by component size and its recall denominator by a maximum such mass; it is not the fixed-label Tatbul overlap/fragmentation target. A component hierarchy gives activation and merge events, but the range metric still requires the label-conditioned recall state, intersection counts, normalization and global precision/recall reducers described here. Moving middle bias also requires split prefix queries; one unsplit count and first moment cannot recover it. An augmented tree implementing those same identities belongs to the same algorithmic class, which the paper correctly admits. Requiring the authors to beat that equivalent realization asymptotically would be an inappropriate novelty test.

My judgment is that this **specific exact algorithm is publishably novel at a modest level**, once paired with evidence that it removes substantial actual work. Its simplicity is not a correctness defect, but it prevents a high originality score. The [VUS 2025 paper](https://www.paparrizos.org/papers/BoniolVLDBJ25.pdf), Klein and Benchopt already establish efficient support/static-work formulations; the VUS part alone would be a weaker regular-paper case. The weighted joint-state result carries this recommendation.

The bibliography discusses fourteen 2024–2026 papers. The structured `literature.json` has twelve entries; it must not be called a separately updated fourteen-row matrix. Several recent papers concern benchmarking, metric axioms or model selection rather than the closest computational mechanism. Their presence supports contextual coverage, not a proof of firstness. The old component-tree and Tatbul antecedents are more decisive for originality.

Importance is sufficient but bounded. The [TimeEval maintainer issue](https://github.com/TimeEval/TimeEval/issues/22) identifies repeated range-metric computation and threshold sampling as a real optimization opportunity. Its current very-low-priority label is also visible; the issue is motivation, not evidence of adoption or urgent industrial demand. Here exact native evaluation expands the query set from 2,776 to roughly 71,750 while the supplemental incremental workflow median changes only from 1.043 to 1.073 seconds on Apple and 1.438 to 1.499 seconds on Linux. That is a useful cost–fidelity observation, not a same-output speed ratio or a demonstrated selection-quality gain. Together with the matched exhaustive speedups, it is enough for a focused operator paper.

## Technical merit and correctness

I found no unresolved mathematical blocker. The recall count update follows from one-dimensional connectivity inside a contiguous true interval. Outside activations cannot join two fragments inside that same interval. The precision update removes at most two neighboring components and adds the replacement; active neighbors of an inactive position are endpoints, so stale interior boundary entries do not invalidate the access. The true-start prefix formula counts intersecting label intervals correctly. Piecewise feature prefixes handle moving endpoints, while recall weights have fixed endpoints. The stated `C_gamma(n)` term avoids hiding arbitrary cardinality computation.

The sorting reduction is valid for input-associated exact query outputs on arbitrary distinct real scores. It does not establish a lower bound for scalar maxima, anonymous outputs or fixed-width radix computation, and the paper now states these limits. Canonical integer moment bounds and finite-feature exact-arithmetic arguments are distinct from floating-point correctness. The implementation's empirical parity and rational finite-set oracles are credible support, but there is no universal rounding certificate. That limitation does not invalidate the stated arithmetic theorem.

The native adapter retains the source query selection, inclusive eligibility through `nextafter`, endpoints, ordering, integration and validation for its explicitly supported default configuration. Public comparisons cover 28/52 profiles per contract; the 24 Stock profiles have compiled-to-compiled agreement, not completed public-reference parity. The manuscript states that restriction. No censored execution is being counted as a numerical success or ordinary completed-time observation.

## Experimental support and reproducibility

The new controls answer a meaningful mechanism question: conventional closed-form interval work does not account for the full observed speedup. The faster raster must still be used when assessing the size of the gain. The independent record calculations above support that interpretation and the retained failure regime. Public-library comparisons remain useful engineering context but are not credited as pure algorithmic improvements.

The protocol chronology is appropriately qualified: follow-ups were frozen before their own measurements, after earlier results and a partial pilot existed. Fresh Linux generation is a reproducibility check on the selected workload, not another independent dataset panel or an isolated CPU intervention. Neither confidence intervals nor case counts should be used to erase these dependencies.

Reproduction support is unusually detailed, with raw/member identities, source revisions, source-bound protocols, saved curves, trial CSVs and separate auditors. The current paper truthfully provides one command for the interval-control tables and separate drivers for the other studies. It does not establish one universal command rebuilding every table. Raw arrays with unresolved redistribution terms stay local and must be reacquired. Those are practical limitations, not evidence of a defective computation. I have verified archived outputs as specified above, not independently completed a new end-to-end replay.

## Largest remaining uncertainty and stopping judgment

The largest uncertainty is **whether an expert board regards the coupled Tatbul state as enough conceptual novelty for a regular Foundations and Algorithms paper**, given the mature component-tree and interval-attribute toolkit. I judge that it clears the bar narrowly because the contribution is explicit, correct, general across a useful declared weight family, and materially effective beyond same-compiler conventional implementations in an actual exact-query consumer. A reasonable reviewer could assign greater weight to its specialization and disagree. This review preserves that uncertainty rather than converting code correctness or artifact volume into a claim of exceptional research significance.

Another finite timing experiment on similar recordings is unlikely to materially change my recommendation. Repeating runs until the larger Linux ratio recurs, or adding cases until MSL loses disappear, would be outcome-chasing. An exact counterexample, a prior implementation containing the same coupled identities, or a concrete stronger equal-output algorithm would change the correctness/novelty judgment. Direct evidence of routine use in a demanding independent consumer could strengthen importance, but it is not a prerequisite I impose after the present controls have answered the stated application question. No further timed study is required for my **Accept, internal 7/10** recommendation.

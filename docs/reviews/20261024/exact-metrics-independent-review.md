## Summary and recommendation

The paper studies exact computation of two established anomaly-evaluation operators. Its VUS result preserves global ties and the ROC predecessor contribution while eliminating irrelevant threshold states. Its new range result maintains label-conditioned overlap, fragmentation, and predicted-component precision under point activations. Both use standard underlying tools. The range contribution is not the discovery of component trees, an exhaustive threshold search, or a new metric; it is the derivation and implementation of the sufficient statistics that avoid repeating a raster scan at every queried threshold.

I recommend **weak acceptance** because the range result now supplies a second, meaningfully different exact operator with a clear same-compiler algorithmic gain. The exhaustive-query improvement over an optimized fused raster is substantial on real stored-score workloads, rather than only over a slow public Python implementation. The unusual care over output contracts, adversarial boundary cases, strong conventional controls, adverse results, and raw curves makes the evidence persuasive within its stated scope. The combined paper has an identifiable contribution beyond a collection of implementation tricks.

The principal reservation is **scientific breadth and practical importance**, not an identified correctness defect. These are two specialized operator derivations, not a general theory of incremental evaluation; the new range experiment reuses the same panel; and only a 1.031-fold application-wide improvement has actually been measured. I would not assign a stronger recommendation on the present evidence. Completing a fresh reproduction closes an evidence gate; it does not by itself make the contribution more conceptually novel.

**Surrogate scale:** 1–2 = fatal/unsupported contribution; 3–4 = reject with major scientific deficiencies; 5 = borderline leaning reject; 6 = revise/borderline, with a material acceptance concern; 7 = weak accept for a sound useful contribution with substantial limits; 8 = clear accept; 9–10 = unusually strong or exceptional contribution. **Score 7/10; confidence 4/5** means high confidence in the inspected mathematics and bounded empirical claims, with residual uncertainty about exhaustive prior-art coverage and transport to other workloads. This is an internal ordinal rubric. The confidential PVLDB CMT form and a public PVLDB score distribution were not available; there is no justified numerical conversion to acceptance odds.

## Strengths and technical assessment

1. **The target is specified precisely enough to review.** The VUS operator uses non-strict thresholds and complete global ties; range-F1 uses strict thresholds. Public 100-grid range-F1, exhaustive observed thresholds, TimeSeAD's alpha-zero classic variant, and float32 storage are separated. The observed-threshold domain deliberately excludes the all-active state below the minimum. The paper does not turn oracle model selection into deployable threshold learning or improved detector accuracy.

2. **The core identities appear correct.** For VUS, removing inactive thresholds requires the horizontal ROC rectangle before the next active event; connecting retained ROC endpoints directly is wrong. The supplied counterexample and event formula distinguish these operations. The two-nearest-interval argument for clipped square-root ramps is valid under the specified ramp and merging conventions. Its fixed-binary64 radix claim is distinguished from comparison sorting.

   For range recall, inserting a point in one true interval changes its overlap mass and fragment count by `1 - active_left_in_interval - active_right_in_interval`. An off-label insertion cannot join fragments inside a single contiguous true interval. Precision removes at most two neighboring component contributions and inserts their replacement, using label-prefix counts for overlap and intersected true intervals. Adjacent active positions are current component endpoints, so stale interior endpoint entries are not read. This supports constant work per activation. The score-sort and query-bucket bounds include their preparation and query-order costs. There is no reliance on monotone recall, which would be invalid here. I found no counterexample to these statements in the specified domain.

3. **The strongest experimental comparison addresses the natural objection.** The fused raster is compiled by the same Numba toolchain, scans once per query, uses constant reducer workspace, and avoids preparation needed only by the candidate. Complete calls include validation, query construction, preprocessing, sorting, curves, and maximizing-value selection. On all 73 cases and 584 full-precision profiles, the range results are:

## Numbered weaknesses

### Major scientific limitations

**W1. The advance is specialized, and its importance is not established by the largest speedup alone.** Classical level-set/component-tree construction already supplies the structural events. [Menotti et al. (2007)](https://hal.science/hal-00622373) directly address the one-dimensional case; their author-uploaded text, inspected in Sections 3.1 and 3.3–3.4, includes a linear construction and component attributes. The final source correctly credits this and its presented `O(max(n,m))` space dependence on integer weight amplitude. Consequently endpoint maintenance itself is not a fresh data-structure contribution. The surviving range result is the particular overlap/cardinality aggregate update; the paper should remain explicit about that distinction. I found no evidence that Menotti or the inspected TimeSeAD implementation already supplies these metric aggregates, but a bounded search cannot prove absence from all earlier work.

The two algorithms also do not constitute a general theorem for arbitrary positional weights, cardinality penalties, temporal metrics, or graph domains. Range updates cannot inherit VUS's off-support pruning. I view this as a useful specialized generalization sufficient for weak acceptance, not a general incremental-metric framework. A paper claiming the latter would need more theory or operators.

**W2. Application-wide significance remains modest and the exact/approximate cost frontier could be clearer.** The unchanged public nine-metric consumer improves only 34.260 to 33.234 seconds (1.031-fold), despite 66.07-fold VUS acceleration, because VUS was 2.7% of baseline cost and affiliation dominates. No new combined VUS-plus-range consumer acceleration has been measured. The manuscript states this honestly. It supports repeated evaluation of stored scores as an operator workload, not a large improvement to complete detector training or general benchmarking pipelines.

Using the saved raw timings, I additionally computed the per-case median distinct-sweep time divided by grid100-bucket time, then its collection geometric mean: **2.190/2.136/3.013 for NAB/SMAP/SMD**. These are different-output comparisons, with no paired same-contract speedup interpretation or new confidence interval. They show that the fastest observed exact route still costs roughly two to three times the fastest sampled route on this panel. The accompanying fidelity result is meaningful—15 nonoverlapping winner sets and 12 regrets at least .01—but it should be read as a cost/fidelity choice. This is a post-hoc reviewer derivation, not a new frozen endpoint. A compact joint cost/fidelity summary from the existing records would make the practical decision easier to assess.

**W3. Generality is limited by reuse and dependence.** The new range panel is the same 73 series/584 score profiles, not fresh held-out evidence. Sixty-two recordings were previously measured by name. Thirty-six SMD files contain nine distinct label sequences; 18 cases share a development label geometry. Equal-label-block analyses are valuable (range distinct ratio 112.34, CI [46.21,167.79]) but do not create independent physical sources. The eight fixed procedures are workload generators, including transductive preprocessing, not eight validated deployment pipelines. Three short public-comparator cases per collection are length-selected and cannot establish population-level speedup factors. Long real-series evidence is available for VUS; range million-point stress checks address numerical behavior, not broad real-data performance.

These limitations narrow external validity without invalidating the paired fixed-panel comparisons. A new frozen, source-disjoint real panel would be the most informative future generality experiment; merely adding more repetitions on these files would not address this issue.

### Evidence and presentation issues

**W4. Combined one-command reproduction and new-mechanism two-host evidence are pending.** The documented command now correctly invokes `reproduce_range_sweep.py --include-vus`, reconstructing numerical JSON rather than promising a freshly typeset manuscript. The original VUS pipeline has a completed fresh Linux run. At this cutoff the new range and combined wrappers have not supplied a completed external-host receipt. Local audits and a running workflow are not substitutes. This is a bounded, falsifiable completion condition, not evidence that the numerical results are false. The current PDF discloses the boundary.

**W5. “Exact” needs its mathematical and numerical qualifiers wherever outputs are summarized.** The abstract says “identical outputs,” whereas measured equivalence is within floating-point tolerance, not bitwise identity. Use “numerically matching outputs” in that sentence. Compensated sums and observed drift do not give a length-independent rounding bound. The TimeSeAD public experiment validates the maximal F1 scalar returned by the chosen consumer; it does not validate every field of TimeSeAD's returned auxiliary dictionary or every degenerate-input convention. Both the strict observed-query domain and pinned TSB all-one compatibility should remain visible in API documentation. No nontrivial-domain mismatch was found in the inspected comparisons.

**W6. The novelty ledger must not imply twelve equally close algorithmic competitors.** The twelve 2024–2026 primary entries include metric definitions, benchmark critiques, model-selection work, and benchmark papers. They support context and scope, while the closest computation competitors are the dynamic VUS algorithms, released exact evaluators, and TimeSeAD's repeated interval evaluation. The final related-work section mostly makes this distinction; keep it explicit. Different-output PATE, CCE, or newer metric proposals cannot substantiate an equal-output speed superiority claim.

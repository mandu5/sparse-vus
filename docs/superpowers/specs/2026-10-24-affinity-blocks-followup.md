# Finite native TimeEval session-block follow-up

Written before this follow-up's measurements on 24 October 2026. It follows
four completed Apple/Linux study executions; it is not a preregistration of
the overall research program. The original Linux exhaustive raster/bucket
ratio was 1.932 and the supplemental Linux ratio 2.756 despite matching score
hashes. Their CPU models were not captured. This motivates one bounded check
of controlled process-session variability, not selection of a favorable run.

## Frozen question and design

Does the exhaustive three-metric native TimeEval workflow retain its compiled
control advantage across five newly started Python processes on one runner,
when input identity, permitted logical CPU, thread settings, warm-up, and
method-order balance are controlled?

- Exactly the same 26 recordings and 52 FFT/COPOD profiles as both earlier
  Linux executions. Generate scores once from the pinned public sources,
  independently check native inputs, and require both prior arrays/CSV hash
  sets to match before measuring. A mismatch is a preparation failure.
- Same native CSV-read, three-metric computation, result-CSV-write/close
  boundary. Flat/cardinality-one range PR-AUC plus ROC-AUC and PR-AUC.
- Compiled raster, closed-form interval, and incremental bucket backends;
  two native contracts, nominal 50 and all nonminimum observed queries.
- Five new child processes, each pinned before numerical imports to the
  same lowest permitted logical CPU. This does not reserve an exclusive
  physical core or remove shared-runner background load. Numerical pool
  sizes and all live thread affinity sets are checked and recorded.
- In each process: fixed tiny JIT warm-up, then one untimed full combined
  workflow per method/contract. Six balanced method permutations per
  contract follow the code-frozen seed and schedule: 36 measured workflows
  per block, 180 total. No public timeout baseline or detector fitting is
  repeated inside blocks.
- Four primary contrasts: raster/bucket and interval/bucket under each query
  contract. Average the six matched log ratios inside each block, then
  equally average five block means. Enumerate all 3,125 empirical bootstrap
  sequences of the five blocks, adjusting tails over these four contrasts.
  These are descriptive small-sample intervals, without guaranteed coverage
  or new-host/population inference. Report all five block estimates.
- Compare saved scalar CSV outputs across methods, repetitions, warm-ups
  and blocks for identical inputs/contracts. Invalid output, final binding,
  receipt, affinity, or process completion invalidates the affected block
  for a complete five-block interval; retain its actual durations and bytes.
  A retained timeout is different from an invalid scalar: it suppresses each
  contrast missing that paired duration, without manufacturing a completed
  runtime or invalidating unrelated completed contrasts by itself.

## Finite stopping and interpretation

Execute one explicitly authorized tagged workflow, with a 90-minute overall
cap and the unchanged 600-second call deadline. Attempt all five blocks even
after a failed child or malformed trace. Keep every observation and outlier;
no retries, replacements, trimming, or stopping on a favorable estimate.
Partial evidence cannot be described as a complete five-block result.

The code/protocol receive an independent pre-measurement audit; a different
reader independently reconstructs the saved results afterward. A stable gain
supports this fixed-runner/fixed-workload robustness claim. An uncertain or
negative result must narrow the paper's practical claim and remain visible.
Neither result changes the four earlier executions, proves a causal reason
for their difference, or establishes greater conceptual novelty. After this
bounded result and final manuscript verification, proceed to the authorized
PVLDB submission rather than repeat measurements to seek a larger ratio.

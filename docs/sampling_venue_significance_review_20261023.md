# Independent significance and venue review, 2026-10-23

This review inspected the theory notes, `paper/sampling_theory.tex`, `sampling.py`, `sampling_bounds.py`, and the frozen MSAD sampling/certificate protocols. It intentionally did not inspect their corpus outcome logs. The recommendation below concerns a scientific story and the evidence it requires; it is not a predicted editorial decision.

## Recommended scientific story

Center the new paper on **reliable computational evaluation of time-series anomaly detectors**: identify how post-saturation soft mass causes a fixed threshold grid to overestimate dynamic VUS-PR, construct substantial selection regret at ordinary buffer widths, and provide an integer-directed certificate for the exhaustive real-arithmetic target. The new twelve-configuration score study then measures when the mechanism matters and whether the certificate makes useful decisions. The existing efficient exact evaluator supplies a practical reference and fallback.

This is a stronger, coherent question than another speed comparison: when a benchmark or model-selection pipeline uses a sampled metric as its target, which decisions remain justified under the exhaustive definition? Numerical agreement, model-selection fidelity, and statistical/operational utility are different claims. Exact VUS regret establishes the second relative to a declared metric; it does not establish which detector has greater real deployment utility.

| Evidence component | What it adds | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing 308-series/three-configuration exact evaluation | Previously measured implementation correctness/cost on its original workload | Reusing it is supporting evidence, not a new independent study |
| New 128-series/twelve-configuration MSAD panel | Outcome-independent selection from a published, much broader score pool; exact-vector identity checks and complete fixed-model comparisons | Not twelve new detector architectures, new dataset families, or a guarantee of distinct physical recordings |
| Post-saturation overshoot construction | A mechanism specific to the declared dynamic soft-label contract, including finite width-30/100/300 ranking witnesses | The huge-length near-unit bound is a worst-case theorem, not a typical benchmark observation |
| Integer-directed certificate | A machine-checkable ordering/enclosure guarantee with explicit integer bounds and outward display conversion | Certifies the ideal real-arithmetic metric, not every implementation's floating-point bit pattern; not automatically an acceleration |
| Matched compiled sampled evaluator | Separates grid error from implementation speed and semantic drift | Agreement with upstream at its configured grid does not make that grid exhaustive |

The fixed primary empirical question is width 30, threshold budget 250, full twelve-model winner sets, and best-case exhaustive regret. The predeclared practical gate (regret at least 0.01 on at least 5% of selected series spanning at least three families) is a study decision rule, not an acceptance threshold. Report its outcome without substituting a better-performing width/budget. Secondary budgets and widths explain sensitivity.

## Venue fit

**TMLR is the most direct current fit** for the reliability-of-evaluation story. Its official criteria ask for accurate, convincing evidence and findings that interest and are clearly communicated to an ML audience. They do not require a new benchmark SOTA. A mere rerun is insufficient when it teaches no transferable lesson; a systematic study with actionable conclusions can qualify. Our proposed mechanism, certified remedy, and selector-target consequences address that distinction. This is a fit judgment, not an easier-review argument. [TMLR acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html)

The same page expressly discusses human readers and says a fully AI-generated paper with little or no human involvement is unlikely to meet the communication criterion. This is not a numerical minimum for human effort or an automatic categorical ban. The author should personally understand the definitions, check the central arguments and experimental interpretation, decide which claims to defend, and revise the manuscript for its intended audience. No tool-generated declaration can substitute for that involvement. Do not attest that these actions happened before they do. [TMLR acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html)

**PVLDB needs a different substantive emphasis.** Its scope policy requires a core data-management contribution, a non-superficial connection to database research, and evaluation in motivating data-management applications. Prior VUS/MSAD publications in database venues do not establish this paper's scope. An efficient, correct evaluation operator over stored score collections could motivate such a paper, but naming an array a database is insufficient. The current sampling-fidelity/certification story fits TMLR more naturally than an unimplemented database query system. [PVLDB scope policy](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html)

If pursuing PVLDB Regular Research, Foundations and Algorithms is a plausible category when the algorithmic contribution and data-management workload are genuinely central. An EA&B submission would need a comprehensive, broadly informative benchmark contribution and its stricter artifact obligations. Regular and EA&B papers have twelve content pages, including appendices; the new theory cannot simply be appended to an already full old manuscript. [PVLDB research categories](https://www.vldb.org/2027/call-for-research-track.html), [submission and artifact rules](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html)

## Evidence needed for a defensible positive recommendation

1. **One explicit semantic target.** State dynamic mass, clipped recall, existence reward, overlap and adjacency treatment, all integer widths, complete global score ties, and right-step PR integration. Compare upstream and matched implementations under that exact contract. Explain which nearby VUS variants differ. Avoid describing a compatible sampled implementation as defective merely because its documented grid is finite.
2. **Theory linked to implementation.** Keep the original underestimation example distinct from the new overshoot mechanism. Check the finite ordinary-width witnesses against independent evaluators. For the certificate, show why square-root brackets enclose, fixed-point accumulation cannot overflow, nonlinear operations use exact Python integers, divisions are directed, and the final dyadic average encloses the ideal target. Clearly state `n < 2**31`, `D=2**32`, and `S=2**56`. A posteriori epsilon agreement alone cannot establish certification. Do not promise nested independently rounded intervals without intersection or prove an adaptive policy that is not implemented.
3. **Full prespecified empirical consequences.** Include all selected series/configurations, failures, both signs of numerical error, tied-winner best/worst regret, strict pair reversals, and practical effect sizes. Keep pooled, equal-family, and per-family results. Family bootstrap intervals summarize this selected collection under the chosen cluster model; they do not turn the collection into an IID population sample. Report ties and score precision because both can control grid behavior.
4. **Useful certificate decisions and honest cost.** The separately frozen accuracy study covers all 128×12 inputs at width 30 and budgets 250/1000: enclosure widths, certified winners, and provably suboptimal sampled winner sets. A fixed thirty-series complete-call supplement should compare certificate cost with exact evaluation, including validation/preparation/integer roots. It may be slower. Report inconclusive intervals and exact fallback directly; a sound but rarely decisive interval remains a diagnostic, not an automatic efficient selector. These are required measurements, not presumed successes.
5. **Matched end-to-end comparisons.** Keep numerical fidelity separate from runtime. Use complete public API calls, identical inputs, serial randomized paired repetitions, source hashes, synthetic warmup, and fixed timeouts. If dense evaluation also computes ROC, say so. Timing ratios on successful complete pairs are conditional when other cases fail; a general comparative claim requires the declared coverage and parity checks to be complete. Retain host interference and censored calls rather than silently discarding them.
6. **A self-contained paper and reusable evidence.** The manuscript must teach the failure mechanism and how to use the certificate without requiring readers to inspect a chat history or every artifact. Bind claims/tables to immutable result hashes, preserve negative cases, and provide clean-environment acquisition/reproduction instructions. Present the author’s actual role and intellectual review accurately.

If the practical primary gate fails, a narrower theory-and-certification paper can still be evaluated on its actual findings. The paper then cannot claim that consequential default-budget ranking errors are prevalent on this corpus. Conversely, passing the gate does not establish general metric superiority, universal error prevalence, or acceptance. No empirical gate replaces the mathematical and presentation checks above.

## Source interpretation discovered during this review

The pinned MSAD `utils/config.py` lists `AE, CNN, HBOS, IFOREST, IFOREST1, LOF, LSTM, MP, NORMA, OCSVM, PCA, POLY`. `ScoresLoader.load` reads every stored vector without a sign change; `compute_single_metric` feeds those vectors directly to ROC/PR evaluation, and the original threshold loop tests `score >= threshold`. This verifies the public consumer’s higher-is-more-anomalous convention across all twelve configurations. It does not by itself identify the exact historical training revision behind every archived vector. [MSAD score loader](https://github.com/boniolp/MSAD/blob/8141b94eb3d2b1811f81ffcf7bc8aa5dcbd6c204/utils/scores_loader.py), [MSAD metric source](https://github.com/boniolp/MSAD/blob/8141b94eb3d2b1811f81ffcf7bc8aa5dcbd6c204/utils/metrics.py)

Crucially, that MSAD metric source uses an older static extended-label normalization and trapezoidal PR calculation. Our new study applies the declared dynamic/right-step target to their released vectors. It must not be described as reproducing or correcting MSAD’s published metric table. The source loader’s generic writer specifies `%.2f`, but that alone does not prove every archived model file used the writer. Preserve the actual stored precision, document direct format checks separately, and do not claim to recover unavailable full-precision scores.

Subsequent input-only verification is recorded in `docs/msad_score_interpretation_20261023.md`: every retained score file has at most two decimal places; twenty selected series have joint observation/label matches after a one-point endpoint removal from prior data. The frozen 128-case panel remains unchanged. An independent primary reporting audit confirms eight practical-regret cases in the full panel, but only four in the 108 cases without this bounded match. These facts strengthen the need for the stated quantization and physical-recording overlap limits; they do not retroactively redefine the primary panel.

Verified bibliographic entry from the pinned README: Emmanouil Sylligardos, Paul Boniol, John Paparrizos, Panos Trahanias, and Themis Palpanas. **Choose Wisely: An Extensive Evaluation of Model Selection for Anomaly Detection in Time Series.** *Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment* 16(11), 2023, 3418–3432. DOI: [10.14778/3611479.3611536](https://doi.org/10.14778/3611479.3611536).

## Suggested new manuscript organization

1. Problem and one interpretable decision example, selected transparently only after the fixed experiment completes; clearly distinguish an illustrative example from the complete empirical distribution.
2. Exact semantic contract and relation to existing implementations.
3. Post-saturation overshoot theorem, finite ordinary-width ranking witnesses, and the distinct role of earlier underestimation results.
4. Enclosure derivation, integer realization, guarantees, and how a practitioner interprets a certified or inconclusive comparison.
5. Prespecified published-score study: input provenance, exact/sampled agreement controls, errors and selection consequences, certificate coverage, and complete-call cost.
6. Efficient exact reference/fallback and the older workload as supporting context, with its chronology and scope explicit.
7. Limits, reproducibility, and actionable guidance: which claim a sampled scalar supports, when certification resolves ordering, and when exact evaluation is needed.

The strongest deliverable is one comprehensible, evidence-bound paper with a reproducible package. Adding many loosely connected experiments or claiming a global SOTA does not substitute for this chain of argument.

## Final empirical and venue review after the frozen runs

Reviewed the updated `paper/certified_sampling.tex`, `paper/sampling_empirical.tex`, and README after completion of the numerical, certificate, and timing studies. The earlier unobserved-outcome review above remains a record of the proposed evidence requirements; the following assessment uses the completed evidence.

The primary result is independently reconstructed in `artifacts/msad-score-provenance-20261023/independent-primary-audit.json`. It confirms the fixed 8/128 practical-regret result and all 2,256 primary numerical controls. The paper now reports the full 20-case endpoint-overlap finding and the weaker 4/108 descriptive subgroup, the limited stored score precision, both signs of integration error, and the distinction from MSAD’s original metric tables. Its explanation that 250 sampled ranks can omit score classes even when there are at most 101 distinct values is consistent with rank sampling and complete-tie semantics.

The strongest practical control is now explicit: full-grid integer certification gives the same rigorous target guarantee, resolves every winner in this selected corpus, and produces exact lower-regret comparisons. The paper does not confuse the 63/128 partial-grid confirmations with detection of the erroneous choices. Its primary integer claim is tied to the independently audited full-grid regret ledger rather than a floating-point epsilon test.

The separate cost study completes all 5,400 scheduled calls and 360 three-way cases. Its independent standard-library audit verifies every timed output against the reference ledgers and reconstructs the report. Median time ratios are 0.826371 for partial/full certification, 2.063173 for partial/numerical exact, and 2.712854 for full/numerical exact. The manuscript accurately distinguishes median ratios, geometric ratios, summed per-case medians, and the 71.918-second whole collection wall time. It discloses that cost-bootstrap intervals were not prespecified and that full-grid accuracy aggregates preceded the final three-way cost freeze. Python 3.12.4, NumPy 1.26.4, Numba 0.59.1, Apple M1 Pro and 16 GiB memory were checked against run receipts/current host metadata. No numerical or cost inconsistency was found in this comparison to the cited ledgers.

The resulting recommendation is coherent: use complete score classes for ordinary numerical evaluation on this release; use full-grid integer intervals when the ideal-target guarantee is needed; treat partial-grid certificates as sufficient tests that may be inconclusive. This is supported by the measured tradeoff. It does not require a claim that partial certification is the fastest evaluator or the best selector.

For **TMLR**, the completed story now has a concrete ML-evaluation audience, a mechanism with finite constructive consequences, an explicit real-arithmetic guarantee, and measured actionable lessons with strong simple controls. That is the appropriate scientific framing under the official evidence/interest/communication criteria. The remaining scientific limits—quantized released scores, bounded source-overlap checks, one-host timing, and the distinction between exact metric regret and deployment utility—are substantive scope restrictions and are now disclosed. They do not become universal conclusions merely because every declared call passed. Actual author intellectual review remains distinct from internal tool/agent checks and must be represented accurately.

For **PVLDB**, the new paper still centers on evaluation fidelity and certification rather than a new data-management system/operator demonstrated in a substantial application workflow. The completed experiments do not change the earlier scope recommendation. TMLR is the direct venue fit for this manuscript; making it a PVLDB submission would require substantive content changes, not a different title or more database citations.

This final internal review identifies no remaining empirical-table or cost-scope correction from its ledger comparison. It supplies a defensible submission framing and bounded claims, not an external reviewer rating or prediction of acceptance.
